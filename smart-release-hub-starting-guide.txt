✦ Since we've converted the app to a Single-Origin Architecture with a Static ngrok Domain, your restart process is now extremely fast and simple.

  The "One-Two" Routine (To go Live):

  Step 1: Start the Backend Server
  Open a terminal and run:

   ./venv/bin/python3 main.py
   
  Wait 2 seconds for it to say Application startup complete.

  Step 2: Start the Permanent Tunnel
  Open a second terminal and run:

   ngrok http 8000 --domain=flashily-pension-wagon.ngrok-free.dev

  ---

  How to access it:

   * For You (Local): Open your browser and go to http://localhost:8000.
   * For Everyone Else (Public): Share your permanent link:
      https://flashily-pension-wagon.ngrok-free.dev
